Constellation Rulers & Rashis

The zodiac according to Indian Astrology comprises 360 degrees. There are 27 Nakshatras or constellations in it. Each Nakshatra is assigned a planetary ruler that determines the native's Dasha at the time of birth, and is mapped to specific Zodiac signs (Rashis).
